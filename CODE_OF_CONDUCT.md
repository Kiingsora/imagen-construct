# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, provide evidence when making technical claims, and make room for participants with different levels of experience.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- constructive and specific feedback;
- patient explanations;
- transparent disclosure of uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- respect for artistic, cultural, and technical differences;
- responsible handling of private reports and user content.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized conduct or imagery in project spaces;
- deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith argument;
- publication of another person's private information;
- submitting stolen, unlicensed, or non-consensual media;
- using project infrastructure to distribute harmful or illegal content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

When a private reporting channel becomes available, it will be listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Until then, avoid including sensitive personal information in public issues.
